## X01 — "17 used mugs"

**Source:** r/pettyrevenge, u/PickledCarrot19 — https://www.reddit.com/r/pettyrevenge/comments/18g6ctq/grumpy_fil_gets_more_mugs_than_he_can_handle/
**Fact outline (from source):** family wanted to give away extra mugs → father-in-law objected it was too wasteful → couple gave him 17 used mugs at the family Christmas → husband told his siblings in advance, everyone knew how their dad was and agreed it'd be funny → one sibling said it was not the time or place → they did it anyway → only the FIL was surprised → he made excuses, husband responded, conversation moved on, no drama.

### Voice script (EN, ~45 s)
1. My father-in-law said giving away our old mugs was wasteful. So for Christmas… he got all of them.
2. We had way too many mugs in our kitchen.
3. So we decided to give the extras away.
4. My father-in-law heard about it. And he did not approve.
5. Giving them away, he said, was just too wasteful.
6. Fine. If they were too good to give away… someone should have them.
7. So my husband and I packed them up. Seventeen mugs. All used.
8. My husband warned his siblings ahead of time.
9. They all knew exactly how their dad was. And they all agreed it would be funny.
10. Well… one of them said Christmas was not the time or place.
11. We did it anyway.
12. Christmas Day. The whole family in one room. He opens the box…
13. Seventeen used mugs.
14. Everyone in that room knew. Only he was surprised.
15. He started making excuses. My husband answered him. And the conversation just… moved on.
16. No fight. No drama. Just one man with seventeen more mugs than he can handle.

**On-screen hook (first 2 s):** "He said giving mugs away was wasteful 🙃"

## X02 — "Order me to buy expensive gifts?"

**Source:** r/MaliciousCompliance, u/rustybathtub — https://www.reddit.com/r/MaliciousCompliance/comments/ra4p5u/order_me_to_buy_your_kids_expensive_gifts_for/
**Fact outline (from source):** siblings believed the couple was well off because they are financially responsible → siblings basically ordered them to buy the nieces and nephews expensive Christmas gifts → couple felt that was unfair → they bought messy, fun gifts: play dough, kinetic sand, slime, water balloons, Nerf guns → author joked it could become a new family Christmas tradition.

### Voice script (EN, ~38 s)
1. My siblings ordered us to buy their kids expensive Christmas gifts. So we did. Just… not the kind they expected.
2. My partner and I are careful with money.
3. And somehow, that convinced my siblings we were rich.
4. So this year, they basically ordered us to buy their kids expensive presents.
5. Being responsible with money had turned us into the family bank.
6. That felt unfair. So we complied. Our way.
7. Play dough.
8. Kinetic sand.
9. Slime.
10. Water balloons.
11. And Nerf guns.
12. Every single gift the kids would love… and every parent would dread cleaning up.
13. They wanted us to spend on their kids. We did.
14. And honestly? This might be our new family Christmas tradition.

**On-screen hook (first 2 s):** "They ORDERED us to buy expensive gifts 🎁"
