# Pilot 01 — illustration prompt and scene mapping

One ImageGen contact sheet was used to keep character designs together. The prompt below applies verbatim to all eight assets, with the scene-to-filename mapping specified in the prompt order. Native sheet: 1774 × 887. Each tile was cropped, scaled proportionally, and feathered onto a softly blurred vertical extension. Final PNG delivery: 1080 × 1920. The sharp scene itself is not natively 1080 pixels across.

```text
Create a single production contact sheet with EXACTLY 8 rectangular illustrations in a strict 4 COLUMN x 2 ROW grid, edge-to-edge panels with NO gaps/borders and no lettering. Each tile is meant to be cropped as a vertical composition. Modern flat 2D editorial digital animation, bold shapes, subtle grain, warm muted navy/teal/mustard/coral lighting and depth. Keep character design identical across all tiles. Woman 35, olive skin, shoulder-length wavy dark brown hair, mustard yellow cardigan and white shirt, small gold hoop earrings. Boyfriend 37, short black hair, subtle stubble, navy bomber, grey shirt, black backpack. Tan dog white chest red collar. VISUAL ORDER left-to-right top then bottom: 1 Airport departures curb, woman by grey car waving boyfriend toward terminal; 2 boyfriend at a busy boarding gate on phone and glancing sideways; 3 worried woman on teal sofa holding phone with tan dog; 4 over shoulder woman checks abstract European flight map on phone; 5 close-up raised eyebrows in warm evening light, worried dog looking up; 6 night kitchen table, woman looking at laptop and phone, stack of maps and coffee; 7 woman at night window with phone at ear, calm firm expression, city lights; 8 woman handing car key and red dog leash to a hand outside frame, supportive redheaded friend in green jacket, dog sitting. Center action in each tile vertically, keep upper and lower margins of each tile less busy for subtitle room. No picture contains letters, readable text, logos, brands, watermarks or framing. Fill all 8 cells. Landscape overall image, 4 column by 2 row grid.
```

01 airport; 02 phone at gate; 03 pet-sitting; 04 flight map; 05 landed message reaction; 06 investigation; 07 decision; 08 return. In 08 the redheaded friend is visibly receiving or reaching for the leash in the generated frame. Treat it as an illustrative cutaway or crop to the keys and leash if the voice-over requires the recipient to remain out of frame. Verify each shot matches the approved source outline before the final edit.
