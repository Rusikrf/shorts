# Pilot 02 — Illustration prompts

Both sheets are split in reading order to PNG 1080×1920. Portrait cells were upscaled with a soft side extension to preserve the artwork. The first sheet served as the reference for the second.

## Scenes 01–05

```text
Generate one cohesive storyboard contact sheet of exactly five distinct illustrations in a 3-column by 2-row perfectly equal grid, reading left to right and top to bottom. Use portrait 2:3 canvas with straight panel boundaries exactly at thirds of width and half the height. Fill every panel edge to edge, NO gutters, borders or panel numbers. Bottom-right sixth cell is blank. The five panels are scenes 1 through 5, in exact order. Flat 2D digital illustration, modern animated-explainer style, clean bold shapes, soft grain texture, warm muted palette (navy, teal, mustard, coral), soft cinematic lighting, simple background with depth. Every panel will be used as a vertical 9:16 scene. Keep faces in the upper middle and keep the top 20% and bottom 25% calm for overlays. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no brands. Characters identical every time: tenant around 28, long straight black hair in a low ponytail, light-brown skin, dark-green apron over a cream sweater, small silver nose stud. Landlord around 40, short brown hair, thick eyebrows, beige cardigan and glasses. His wife around 35, visibly pregnant, auburn bob and lilac top.
1. A cozy basement apartment entrance at the bottom of stairs, an open doorway upstairs reveals a shared kitchen. The tenant carries a grocery bag down the stairs.
2. First evening in the shared kitchen. The tenant hands the landlord and his pregnant wife a dish of creamy shrimp pasta. The couple looks pleased.
3. The tenant cooks alone, with two salmon fillets in a skillet and a bowl of green cilantro-lime rice, steam in warm light.
4. The landlord bends and opens the oven door, peering inside with raised eyebrows. The shocked tenant stands behind him holding a spatula.
5. The landlord points at the tenant, lecturing. His pregnant wife is behind him with a hand on her belly. The tenant holds a plate defensively.
```
