## What this says (evidence, not opinion)
1. **Length.** Every Reddit-story hit above 70K is a *full* story of **2:30–3:00** (the Shorts maximum). Ours were 0:15–1:03, often cut off. This is the biggest gap.
2. **Hook = the Reddit post card.** First 2–3 s show a post card (avatar, title, fake likes/comments) while the voice reads the title. The title is a mid-sentence teaser with a concrete detail: "$40 for emotional labor", "160 buses", "Good luck cashing those fifty thousand worthless shares", "11 minutes".
3. **Captions = one big word at a time**, centred, white with dark stroke (some highlight words in colour).
4. **Background = real "oddly satisfying" footage** (cleaning, cutting, slime, chocolate, machines), not games and not illustration. It holds the eye without competing with the story.
5. **Animation is not what wins in this niche.** Cheap/AI animation got 200–1.4K views. Animation wins only at studio quality (3D series) or Roblox-style with a running character — high effort.
6. **Titles** are in-medias-res quotes or numbers, often ending with "…".
7. **Auto-dubbing** (YouTube feature) gives extra reach in other languages for free.

## Recommendation for the pilot
Make **two pilots of the same story** and compare on TikTok + YouTube:
- **Pilot A — "winning format"**: 2:00–2:45 full story, Reddit-style card hook, 1-word captions, satisfying footage, better voice. Fast to make, no images needed.
- **Pilot B — "illustrated"**: same audio, 6–8 consistent illustrations (ChatGPT) with camera motion, matching each scene.
Whichever keeps more viewers (% stayed, avg % viewed) becomes the weekly format.
